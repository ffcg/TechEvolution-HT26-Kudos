# Workflow — what each session is really testing

## Build

Visible output: a working app. Actually tested: whether you read
`.ai/domain-model.md` closely enough to make deliberate calls on its open
questions, instead of guessing silently. See `docs/01-build.md`.

## Explain

Visible output: a walkthrough of your own app. Actually tested: whether you
can tell, with a few hours' distance, what was a deliberate choice versus what
nobody thought about — and defend it to a team that wasn't there when you made
it. See `docs/02-explain.md`.

## Improve

Visible output: a shipped requirement. Actually tested: whether you read the
requirement fully and agreed on an interpretation before anyone opened an
editor. See `docs/03-improve.md`.

## Across all three

Can you say why, not just what — why the code works the way it does, why you
read a requirement the way you did, why a decision is yours to defend rather
than something a tool produced and you accepted.
